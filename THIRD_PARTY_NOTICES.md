Copyright (c) 2025 Jesse Vincent. The complete upstream MIT notice is retained at [superpowers-MIT.txt](skills/brainstorming/licenses/superpowers-MIT.txt). ## Reproducibility matrix definitions

Barba (2018), *Terminologies for Reproducible Research*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03311>. Workflow prose, decision diagrams, and verification scripts are original MIT-licensed material.
